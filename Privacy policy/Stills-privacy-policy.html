Privacy Policy
Last updated: June 13, 2026

Overview

STLLS is built privacy-first. The creative work you make in the app — your photos, videos, and layouts — stays on your device. The only information that ever leaves your device is what you choose to send us: an optional newsletter signup or a feedback message. Even then, it is minimal and is never used for advertising, profiling, or tracking.

Information You Choose to Share

Newsletter. If you opt in — by entering your email and agreeing on the welcome screen, or by turning on “Email updates” in Settings — we collect your name and email address so we can send occasional updates about STLLS. This is entirely optional, requires your consent, and you can unsubscribe or turn it off at any time. Signups are received through Google Forms (Google LLC).

Feedback. If you send feedback from within the app, the message text is submitted anonymously. We do not attach your name, email, or any identifier to it. Feedback is received through Google Forms (Google LLC).

We never sell this information, and we never share it with advertisers or data brokers.

What Stays on Your Device

  • Photos, videos, and the layouts you create
  • Your name (used to greet you) — unless you opt into the newsletter
  • App settings and preferences

Data We Do Not Collect

  • Usage data or analytics
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers
  • Advertising or tracking data of any kind

Photo & Video Library Access

STLLS requests access to your photo and video library solely to allow you to import media and export your finished layouts. This access is used only on your device. No media is ever transmitted to external servers.

Third-Party Services

STLLS uses Google Forms (Google LLC) to receive optional newsletter signups and feedback. Information you submit through those forms is processed by Google; its handling is governed by Google’s Privacy Policy. STLLS does not use any analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

Your Choices

You can opt out of the newsletter at any time by turning off “Email updates” in Settings or using the unsubscribe option in any email. To have your email removed from our list, or to ask any question about your data, contact us at the address below.

Children's Privacy

STLLS is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information — including newsletter signups — from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us their information, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected with an updated date at the top of this page.
