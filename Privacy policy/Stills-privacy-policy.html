Privacy Policy

Overview

STLLS is designed with your privacy in mind. We do not collect, store, or share your personal data. All content you create in STLLS stays on your device.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • Personal information of any kind
  • Usage data or analytics
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers
  • Photos, videos, or media used within the app

Photo & Video Library Access

STLLS requests access to your photo and video library solely to allow you to import media and export your finished layouts. This access is used only on your device. No media is ever transmitted to external servers.

Third-Party Services

STLLS does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Children's Privacy

STLLS does not knowingly collect any data from users of any age.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected with an updated date at the top of this page.
